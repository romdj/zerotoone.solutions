## Analysis & Recommendations

### Common Patterns in Improvements

1. **Lead with outcome/impact** before diving into technical approach
2. **Add scale indicators** where possible (users, devices, global, etc.)
3. **Reduce jargon** or explain it with context
4. **Show business context** not just technical work
5. **Clarify role** (led, built, architected) early
