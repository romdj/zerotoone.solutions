## Purpose & Ethos

### Primary Purpose
Establish immediate credibility and identity as a **multi-domain builder** who works across enterprise platforms, venture companies, and experiments.

### Page Ethos
**"We don't just advise—we build. Here's proof across every domain."**

- First impression: Professional builder with enterprise credibility
- Show range immediately (not just consulting)
- Create intrigue about ventures without overwhelming
- Make self-selection easy (enterprise vs venture vs experiment)

---

## Target Audiences

### Primary (50%): Enterprise Buyers
**Needs:** Solution architect for transformation projects
**First Impression:** "They've built for Nike/IBM—they can handle our scale"
**Key Element:** Trusted Partners section (Nike, IBM, Philips logos)
**Path:** Homepage → Portfolio (consulting) → Services → Contact

### Secondary (30%): Startup Founders
**Needs:** Technology strategy or venture building partner
**First Impression:** "They've done enterprise AND build ventures—they get both sides"
**Key Element:** Venture companies preview (aerowheelcase, tempsdarret.studio)
**Path:** Homepage → Portfolio (ventures) → Services → Contact

### Tertiary (20%): Venture Partners/Investors
**Needs:** Co-building opportunities or incubator access
**First Impression:** "Multidisciplinary builder with proven execution"
**Key Element:** "What We Build" showing full range
**Path:** Homepage → Portfolio (ventures) → Studio → Contact

## Key Messages

### Hero Message
**Primary:** "We Build Solutions—From Code to Companies"

**Why This Works:**
- "We Build" (not "we consult" or "we advise") - active, hands-on
- "Solutions" (not "software" or "products") - outcome-focused
- "From Code to Companies" - shows range without listing everything

**Supporting:**
- "Multi-domain builder combining enterprise-grade architecture with entrepreneurial execution"
- Shows it's not just one thing—it's a capability applied across domains

### Trust Signal
**"Built For:"** Nike | IBM | Philips | Levi's | Engie | ABVV-FGTB

**Why This Works:**
- Recognizable brand names (immediate credibility)
- Mix of industries (not just tech consulting)
- "Built For" (not "worked with") - emphasizes building

### Value Proposition
**"Sometimes we build platforms for Nike and IBM. Sometimes we build our own ventures. Always, we build with our hands."**

**Why This Works:**
- Honest about duality (consulting + ventures)
- "With our hands" = hands-on, not just strategy
- "Always" = consistent builder identity

## Tone & Voice

### Do's ✅

**Confident without arrogance:**
- ✅ "We've built platforms for Nike and IBM"
- ✅ "8+ years building enterprise-grade systems"

**Inclusive language:**
- ✅ "Enterprise platform? New venture? Something in between?"
- ✅ "Let's build something together"

**Builder-first identity:**
- ✅ "We build solutions"
- ✅ "Built for Nike, IBM, Philips"

**Honest about duality:**
- ✅ "Sometimes consulting, sometimes ventures"
- ✅ "It's all building"

### Don'ts ❌

**Avoid consultant language:**
- ❌ "We provide consulting services"
- ❌ "We offer solutions"

**Don't overclaim:**
- ❌ "World's best solution architect"
- ❌ "We've built everything"

**Don't confuse with too much detail:**
- ❌ Listing all 20 technologies in hero
- ❌ Explaining entire journey on homepage

**Don't hide the ventures:**
- ❌ "Primarily a consultancy with some side projects"
- ❌ Making ventures seem like hobbies

## Common Questions & Answers

### Q: "Why lead with 'We Build' instead of 'About' or 'Services'?"
**A:** It establishes identity immediately. Most consultancies say what they do ("we provide X service"). We say who we are ("we're builders"). This is more memorable and differentiating.

### Q: "Won't 4 categories overwhelm visitors?"
**A:** No—they're self-selecting. Enterprise buyers click Enterprise Platforms. Venture partners click Venture Companies. Everyone finds their path without navigating a complex menu.

### Q: "Should we hide ventures to avoid confusing enterprise buyers?"
**A:** No. The ventures PROVE we build, not just advise. Enterprise buyers appreciate that we eat our own dog food. And showing range demonstrates capability.

### Q: "What if someone doesn't understand the multidisciplinary stuff?"
**A:** That's fine. The philosophy preview is for engaged visitors who want to go deeper. Tactical buyers can ignore it and go straight to Portfolio → Services → Contact.

### Q: "How do we balance 'consulting pays bills' with 'ventures are vision'?"
**A:** Equal visual weight on homepage. The transition story lives in /studio (for those who want to understand the journey). Homepage just shows: "We build both."
