## Engagement Models (Optional Detail)

### Enterprise Consulting
**Typical Engagement:**
- Assessment & strategy: 2-4 weeks
- Architecture design & roadmap: 4-8 weeks
- Hands-on implementation: 3-12 months
- Knowledge transfer & team leadership

**Pricing Philosophy:**
- Project-based or time & materials
- Focused on outcomes, not hours
- Transparency on costs and timeline

### Startup Advisory
**Typical Engagement:**
- Monthly retainer (ongoing strategic guidance)
- Project-based (specific architecture design)
- Fractional CTO services available
- Equity + cash (case-by-case)

**Pricing Philosophy:**
- Accessible for seed-stage (not enterprise rates)
- Can include equity component
- Focus on long-term relationship

### Venture Building
**Typical Engagement:**
- Co-founding arrangements (equity-based)
- Joint venture structures
- Build-operate-transfer models
- Incubator program (see below)

**Pricing Philosophy:**
- Equity partnerships (we invest time/expertise)
- Revenue share models
- Depends on venture maturity and fit

### Incubator
**Typical Engagement:**
- Application-based (selective)
- 3-6 month initial engagement
- Hands-on involvement (not just advisory)
- Can lead to co-founding if aligned

**Pricing Philosophy:**
- Varies by situation (some equity, some advisory fee)
- Not a traditional accelerator (no standard equity ask)
- Case-by-case based on fit and commitment

## Tone & Voice

### Do's ✅
- **Flexible language:** "Whatever way makes sense"
- **Builder identity:** "We build alongside you"
- **Proof-based:** Reference Nike, IBM, current ventures
- **Honest about models:** Explain equity vs. retainer vs. project

### Don'ts ❌
- **Consultant jargon:** "Leverage synergies" or "add value"
- **Forcing one model:** Pushing consulting on everyone
- **Hiding costs:** Being vague about engagement structure
- **Overselling:** "We can do everything for everyone"

## Common Questions & Answers

### Q: "How do I know which service I need?"
**A:** Not sure? That's okay. Use the contact form and tell us what you're building. We'll figure out the right approach together.

### Q: "Do you only work with enterprises?"
**A:** No—we work with enterprises (Nike, IBM), startups (seed to Series A), and we build our own ventures. We adapt to what you need.

### Q: "What's the difference between Startup Advisory and Incubator?"
**A:** Advisory is ongoing strategic guidance (you're building, we're advising). Incubator is hands-on co-building (we're in it with you). Depends on your stage and needs.

### Q: "Do you take equity?"
**A:** Case-by-case. For venture building and some incubator engagements, yes. For consulting and advisory, typically no (but can discuss hybrid models).
