## Article Structure Template

### Standard Article Format

**Title:** Action-oriented (not generic)
- ✅ "Counter-Positioning: When to Go Against the Grain"
- ❌ "Thoughts on Strategy"

**Excerpt:** 2-3 sentences (appears in cards and meta description)

**Hero Image:** Relevant, high-quality (not stock photos)

**Content Structure:**
1. **Opening Hook:** Start with a story or problem
2. **Context:** Why this matters
3. **Deep Dive:** The meat (case study, framework, analysis)
4. **Takeaways:** Practical lessons (bulleted)
5. **Application:** How readers can use this
6. **CTA:** Related services or contact

**Length:** 1200-2000 words (deep enough to be valuable, not exhaustive)

**Tone:** Educational, not promotional. Show expertise through insight, not claims.

## Newsletter Strategy

### Platform Options
- **Mailchimp:** Easy, free tier, standard features
- **ConvertKit:** Creator-focused, good automation
- **Substack:** Simplest, but less customizable
- **Buttondown:** Minimal, markdown-focused

**Recommendation:** ConvertKit or Mailchimp (professional, good deliverability)

### Newsletter Frequency
- **Not on a schedule** (1-2x per month when we publish)
- **Promise:** "No spam. Just insights when we have something worth saying."

### Newsletter Content
- New article announcements
- Case study previews
- Framework releases
- Venture updates (aerowheelcase progress, etc.)

## Tone & Voice

### Do's ✅
- **Educational:** Share frameworks, not just opinions
- **Specific:** Use real examples (Nike, Dropbox, aerowheelcase)
- **Practical:** Give takeaways readers can apply
- **Honest:** Admit what didn't work, not just successes

### Don'ts ❌
- **Promotional:** Don't turn articles into sales pitches
- **Generic:** No "10 tips for success" listicles
- **Jargon-heavy:** Explain technical concepts clearly
- **Frequent:** Don't publish for the sake of publishing
